# VR v1392 (release, 2026-11-17)

## Carrying bodies is now easy

- Hold the trigger on a body lying on the ground and it goes onto your shoulder (the game's own carry).
- A body that still has loot is looted first, then carried, while you keep holding the trigger.
- A quick trigger tap still just loots. The grip still grabs and drags the body by hand.
- Use an empty hand: a hand holding a weapon keeps its trigger for the weapon.

## Crosshairs (from the previous release)

- The game's crosshair is whiter and easier to see; the dual-wield second gun has its own white ring crosshair.

## Known issues

- Sometimes the weapon wheel selects a different weapon than the one you pointed at. Choose again if the wrong one
  comes to the hand.

Trigger carry tested by the author in the headset. Say what you see if something is off.
