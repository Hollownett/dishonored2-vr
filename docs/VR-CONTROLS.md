# VR controls and gestures

Grip is the side button under your middle finger, trigger the one under your index finger. Fighting, blocking and grabbing need no buttons: you do them with your hands.

<!-- vr-page:body|Body -->

## Where things are
- **Right temple** — squeeze the right grip to open this guide.
- **Over either shoulder** — squeeze that hand's grip to draw the sword; release there to sheathe.
- **Back of either wrist** — turn it toward your face to read health and mana.
- **Left temple** — squeeze either grip to open the journal.
- **Either hip** — squeeze that hand's grip to draw the pistol or crossbow; release to holster.

<!-- vr-page:controllers|Controllers -->

## Left controller
- **Y** — talk; hold for the pause menu.
- **X** — hold for the left hand's wheel; point, release to equip in that hand.
- **Stick** — move where you look; click to sprint.
- **Menu** — pause; hold for the journal; flick the right stick for quick slots.
- **Trigger** — talk or pickpocket with a free hand; otherwise fire or cast.
- **Grip** — use or hold; hold at a keyhole to peek.

## Right controller
- **B** — drink a health elixir.
- **A** — hold for the right hand's wheel; point, release to equip in that hand.
- **Stick** — turn; push up to jump (hold to swim up or climb); push down to crouch (hold to dive while swimming); click to zoom.
- **Trigger** — select in menus; talk or pickpocket; hold with the sword to charge a strike.
- **Grip** — use or hold; over your shoulder, draw the sword.

## The sword
<!-- vr-figure:sword_draw_block|Grip over your shoulder to draw the sword, then hold the blade in front of you to block. -->
- **Swing** — attack. Behind an unaware guard it is a silent takedown.
- **Hold the trigger with the sword drawn** — charge a power strike (a pulse rises in the hand); release to swing it. A fast swing cancels the charge.
- **Hold it in front of you** — block. No button; a swing ends it. A quick short push into the enemy's swing parries.
- **Let go mid-swing** — throw it. Grip next to it to pick it up.
- **Grip over your shoulder** — call a thrown sword back.

## Your hands
<!-- vr-figure:enemy_grab|Palm on the guard: grip to hold, then pull hard away from him. -->
- **Trigger on a person's prompt** — Talk, Pickpocket, or Loot on a lying body: the trigger does what the game's prompt says. The grip never does, so it will not become a choke by accident.
- **Grip a door or keyhole and keep holding** — peek through; let go to stop.
- **Palm on a guard + grip, pull hard** — throw him down.
- **Grip a guard's neck from behind** — choke him out.
- **Grip a lying body (dead or unconscious), no exact touch needed** — your palm within about a hand's width of any part takes hold of it; drag it; lift it to a shoulder and let go to put it on your shoulder. From there it is the game's own body carry (walk, climb, open doors with the free hand), so a place that expects a carried body (Sokolov out of the Jindosh mansion) sees one. Squeeze at the shoulder and move that hand away to put it down; a throwing motion throws it. A body you have not looted yet is looted as it goes onto the shoulder, then carried (or loot it on the floor with the trigger).
- **Grenade or mine: make a fist** — open it mid-throw; palm on a wall to place it.
