# v646 test build: free-hand pickups with the sword, stealth kills from behind, heavy props, thumb on the ball, cutscene hands

Headset status: not run. Built on v642. User feedback on v642 (headset, 2026-10-27): "everything else finally works as expected"; four items plus the thumb.

## Headset checks

1. **Free hand with the sword.** Draw the sword and hold it in front of you. With the LEFT hand's grip alone, pick up:
   - a severed body part;
   - a bottle or a glass;
   - a coin or other loot.

   Each should come into the left hand. Let go and the sword blocks again. Sneak behind a calm guard and grab his neck with the left hand: the choke still starts.
2. **Stealth from behind.** Sneak up to a calm guard and stab him in the middle of the BACK, not the neck: he dies at once, with a strong pulse. On another guard, slash from the side: same. A guard who has seen you takes an ordinary hit.
3. **Heavy objects.** Grab a crate, a chair or a barrel (grip + trigger) and lift it with your forearm level: it comes up and follows the hand, still feeling heavy. Light props (books, bottles) should feel unchanged.
4. **Thumb.** Hold a book, a stamp, a candlestick: the thumb rests on the outside of the grip, opposite the fingers, never inside the palm. Say if a small object's thumb looks wrong.
5. **Cutscenes.** In an in-engine cutscene, look at your hands and down at your body: if the scene uses your character, your tracked hands and body stay. Report which scenes still show only the game's own arms, for example the Dreadful Wale waking scene. That scene uses its own cinematic model.
