# VR v1391 (release, 2026-11-17)

## Crosshairs

- The game's crosshair is whiter, so it is easier to see.
- New second crosshair for dual wield: a small white ring shows where the gun in your other hand is aiming.

## Carrying bodies on the shoulder (work in progress)

- Bring a body up to your shoulder and the game's own carry takes it over; you don't have to let go.
- Still being worked on to make it easier. If the carry does not start, put the body to your shoulder again.

## Known issues

- Sometimes the weapon wheel selects a different weapon than the one you pointed at. Choose again if the wrong one
  comes to the hand.

Crosshairs checked in simulation; the shoulder carry change is not headset-tested yet. Say what you see if something is off.
