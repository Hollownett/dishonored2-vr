Combat

You fight with your hands, and the game resolves every hit itself: the mod detects contact and calls the game's own damage function, so damage, bleeding, alarms and kills behave as they do on a gamepad.

PLATE I Built

Sword swings

Swing the sword in your right hand. When the blade passes through a guard fast enough, the guard takes the same damage as a gamepad slash, with blood and sound.

Hit speed
Blade tip at 1.5 m/s or faster
Reach
Blade length plus 35 cm past the tip, so near-misses count
Per guard
One hit every 450 ms, so one swing never counts twice
Small creatures
Wasps and rats count within 15 cm of the blade
Walls and props
The blade stops at walls with sparks and dust; loose props get pushed

How it works: a sphere sweep along the blade each frame, then the game's own damage call on the game thread. Fast swings also trigger the native slash, with presses spaced so the game never drops one.

PLATE II Built

Blocking and reverse grip

Hold the sword anywhere in front of you, still or moving, and you block. Only an attack press releases the block. Normal or reverse grip is decided by how your hand takes the hilt, as in Boneworks: there is no button for it.

Block
Sword in front of the chest, any angle (your choice from the headset sessions)
Reverse grip
Fist pointing down when you draw over the shoulder, or a hand against the blade when you pick it up from the floor (v406)
New in v398
While the pistol or crossbow is drawn, the sword does not block. The game puts the gun away to block, which made the pistol unusable. Put the gun away to block again.
PLATE III Built

Stealth takedowns

Come up behind a guard who has not seen you. A quick sideways slash of the right hand near his neck is a lethal takedown, played by the game's own assassination. Hold the right grip or trigger instead to choke him unconscious.

Kill
Sideways slash near neck height (a normal swing also works)
Choke
Right grip or trigger held
Indicator
One small mark near the guard, sized by the HUD size setting
Forgiving
Wider takedown distance (x1.5) and a 1 s hold through sensor flicker
PLATE IV Built

Pistol and crossbow

Draw the pistol or crossbow into your left hand from the wheel and aim by pointing the gun. The crosshair and the shots follow the gun's own barrel, read from the drawn model every frame, so the crosshair sits where the muzzle points on any controller. Once you grip it, the gun is held only while you squeeze the left grip; let go and it goes to your left hip. Squeeze at the left hip to draw it again.

Fire
Left trigger, with the gun in the hand (a bare fist never fires)
Holster
Left hip. An empty left hand's trigger does not pull the gun out (the game would draw it, and the sword too)
Aim source
The gun model's barrel axis (v397). Before, the crosshair followed the controller ray and drifted from the muzzle
Fine trim
%LOCALAPPDATA%\DishonoredVR\build\aim-offset → "yaw pitch" in degrees, default 0 0
Powers
Same left-hand crosshair, along the hand when no gun is drawn

Body and objects

Your hands are tracked into Corvo's or Emily's own arms, measured to your body, and either hand can pick things up.

PLATE V Built

Holding and throwing objects

Squeeze a grip near a bottle, glass, severed arm or loose prop and it sits in that hand. Swing and let go to throw it with your hand's speed; let go gently to drop it. A bottle hitting a guard uses the game's own stun.

Throw or drop
Hand speed at release: 1.2 m/s or faster throws, slower drops
Loose props
Crates, chairs and the like follow your hand physically and keep their weight
Reach
Grab within 45 cm, held up to 55 cm
PLATE VI Built

Body gestures and calibration

Reach over your right shoulder and squeeze the grip to draw the sword; keep squeezing to hold it and let go over the shoulder to sheathe it. Touch your left temple and squeeze to open the journal. Turn the back of a wrist toward your face to read health and mana on a small watch.

Calibrate avatar
VR Settings → Calibrate avatar: hold your hands at your shoulders, then arms straight out, copying the masked Corvo shown
Arms
Both arms stay visible, including the left arm lowered to your waist

Interface

Menus are the game's own screens, shown as panels about a metre in front of you; nothing is a look-alike.

PLATE VII Built

Menus and VR Settings

VR Settings is a page in the game's own pause menu, with the game's sliders and toggles. Tabbed screens (Options › Controls, Visuals, Gameplay, and the Black Market's categories) change one tab per grip squeeze: right grip forward, left grip back.

VR Settings rows
Resolution, anti-aliasing, edge smoothing, monitor view, sharpening, HUD size, controls hint, turning, calibrate avatar, recenter view
Why one per squeeze
The game repeats a held shoulder button after 225 ms, every 75 ms; a natural squeeze used to skip tabs. Since v396 a squeeze in a menu is one tap
Move a menu
Squeeze the left grip to bring it to where you look
Black Market
Held in your left hand like a note (a metre ahead if that hand is not tracked), the whole page with the purchase and back rows; point with the right hand
Launcher
One file, DishonoredVRLauncher.exe: checks, launch, settings, tools

Physical interaction Partly built

The goal is Bonelab-style immersion: weapons you can drop, pick up and throw, and bodies you can drag by a wrist or an ankle. The research found that the game's own Havok physics already has most of the parts.

PLATE VIII Found

Anatomy of a guard

Every guard carries a ragdoll of 17 physics bodies, one per body part. While he is alive they follow his animation. On a corpse they are loose physics bodies lying on the floor, the same kind of body the mod already grabs and throws as a prop.

Alive
Bodies follow the animation
Corpse
Bodies are loose and simulated
Grab points
Hands 7 and 10 (wrists), feet 13 and 16 (ankles), chest 2 and neck 3 (collar)
Engine
Havok physics; each guard keeps the list of its 17 bodies in its physics component
PLATE IX Built · v404

Dragging bodies

Squeeze a grip at a corpse's or unconscious guard's wrist, ankle or collar and walk away. Your hand pulls that one body part, and the ragdoll's joints drag the rest along the floor. Let go to drop it, or swing and let go to fling a light body.

Pull
The prop-grab motor on the gripped limb, plus a tow on the pelvis so the whole body follows (simulator: dragged 5.9 m)
Fallback
A strength-limited joint between your hand and the limb, as in the Skyrim VR mod HIGGS
Carry instead
The game's shoulder carry stays available, as today
PLATE X Built · v411

Dropping and picking up weapons

Open your hand and the sword falls with your hand's motion; open it during a swing and it flies. Reach down, squeeze, and it is back in your hand. The game has no "drop weapon" action, so the mod drops the game's own physical sword prop and instantly puts the real sword away until you pick the copy up. The pistol has no physical prop, so letting go of it sends it back to the hip.

Safety
Squeeze over the shoulder to call a dropped sword back at once. It also returns by itself after 30 s or 12 m. Nothing is locked, so a save never keeps the hand empty
Look
The dropped sword is the game's howler sword prop, not Corvo's own model
Same as
HIGGS for Skyrim VR and ROCK for Fallout 4 VR: drop as a world object, pick up to re-equip
PLATE XI Built · v419 (sword)

Throwing weapons at guards

Swing and let go to throw the sword. It tumbles end over end, glances off walls and lands flat. When it meets a guard, the game's own sword damage is applied, so he reacts and calls out, and the blade drops beside him. The pistol has no physical model, so letting go of it returns it to the hip.

Release speed
Your hand's movement over the last 60 ms, times 1.5. A snap turn or a teleport restarts the measurement
Flight
The mod flies the sword itself (gravity, spin, the game's own world sweep). A sword spawned at runtime is never simulated by the game's physics
Hit test
The flight path and the blade against a body-sized capsule around each guard, once per guard per throw
Damage
The player sword's own damage (one sword hit), from the direction of flight
PLATE XII Built · v427

Grabbing living guards

Grab a living guard by the chest, collar or arm and yank hard: he drops into the game's own ragdoll, the one Far Reach and dropped bodies use, falls toward your pull, and the game stands him back up a few seconds later. He knows who did it.

Guard alive→Grip a limb→Game ragdoll→Release→He gets up
Your choice
"Grab + yank to ragdoll" (2026-10-13)
How
A free hand squeezing at his upper body and pulling faster than 2.2 m/s. The game's shove reaction (no damage), then its START_RAGDOLL with your pull
Simulator
Pulled about 2 m, on the floor for about 2 s, standing again at 4 s and walking at 6 s
Stealth rule
Behind an unaware guard the right grip stays the choke. The left hand can still yank
Holding on
The squeezing hand keeps hold of a limb only when the game exposes his ragdoll bodies; otherwise he just falls
PLATE XIII Planned · Phase 5

Hands that close on things

Fingers curl until each fingertip meets the held object's shape, so a bottle, a sword hilt and a guard's wrist are each held differently. Your hands also get simple physics bodies, so they can push props and doors.

Fingers
Per-finger curl stopped by the object's collision box (HIGGS method)
Hands
Push loose objects; the hand appears on the held object and lets go past about 0.7 m of drift

Build order

Each phase gets a simulator scenario first, then your headset check.

  1. Map a guard's ragdoll. List the 17 bodies, their handles and bone order in the running game, without changing anything.
    Done
  2. Drag bodies by wrist, ankle or collar with the existing prop-grab motor.
    Built · sim
  3. Hold to hold: sword on the back, pistol on the left hip, drop and pick up the sword, grip decided by the hand.
    Built · sim
  4. Throw the sword at guards for damage: it tumbles, glances off walls, lands flat and wounds a guard it meets.
    Built · sim
  5. Throw props at guards: the game's own physics damage (big stagger, 2-8 hp by speed); bottles keep the game's own throw.
    Built · sim (decl verified)
  6. Hands: solid open hands swat props; a holding hand closes by the object's size (per-finger contact is still planned).
    Built · sim
  7. Grab living guards: yank one into the game's ragdoll; he gets up by himself.
    Built · sim
