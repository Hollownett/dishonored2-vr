# VR v1386 (release, 2026-11-16)

[![Dishonored 2 VR v1386 performance mode demo](https://img.youtube.com/vi/Xe4S_abZPgk/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xe4S_abZPgk)

Performance mode demo, watch on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xe4S_abZPgk

## Fixed

- The game no longer freezes after Stilton's mission or after loading a save from the menu.
- No more crash when raising the Timepiece in a level after its mission.

## Weapons and dual wield

- Held weapons stay steady in the hand: no shaking or jumping when moving, jumping or climbing stairs.
- Each weapon is held with its own grip; fingers sit on the pistol's trigger.
- A second (dual-wield) weapon gets the bolt you chose for that hand, and its attachments stay on it.

## Performance mode

- No longer marked experimental. The crossbow no longer doubles while walking.
- With AA Off, SMAA or TAA it now runs as FSR 3 Native, which is easier on the eyes.
- It can still show some visual artifacts, mostly around moving hands and objects.

## Held things and the Timepiece

- Carried bottles and other items no longer jump, shrink or grow; grabbed props work in either hand.
- The Timepiece lens image is steady, with its shadows in the right places.

## Known issues

- Sometimes the weapon wheel selects a different weapon than the one you pointed at. Choose again if the wrong one
  comes to the hand.

Headset-tested by the author. Say what you see if something is off.
